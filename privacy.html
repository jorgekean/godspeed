Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Godspeed Grader

Godspeed Grader is built from the ground up to protect student privacy and teacher data. Your educational records belong solely to you and your school.

100% Local Storage

All students, classes, and exam grades live exclusively on your device's local database. No central server stores your data.

On-Device Computer Vision

Camera frames are processed in real-time via WebAssembly on your phone. No photos or videos are ever uploaded.

No Trackers or Ads

Zero third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or data brokers. Your educational workflow is strictly private.

1 Student Data Privacy & FERPA / COPPA Alignment

Godspeed Grader does not collect, transmit, sell, or rent student personal data. All student identifiers, rosters, and test scores remain confined within your device's sandboxed storage. Because the application does not transmit student data to developer servers, it aligns with student privacy standards, including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2 Camera Permission & Video Feed Processing

When you grant camera access, the camera feed is used exclusively for scanning bubble answer sheets in real-time. The computer vision analysis is executed completely on-device using a client-side WebAssembly build of OpenCV.

  • Camera frames are evaluated in volatile memory and discarded immediately after mark detection.
  • No photos, video streams, or biometric faces are ever recorded, saved, or sent over the internet.
  • You can revoke camera permissions at any time in your device’s System Settings.

3 Optional Cloud Storage & Apple Files / iCloud Drive

Godspeed Grader provides optional cloud and file backup features strictly under your control:

  • Apple Files & iCloud Drive: When you export a backup, the file is saved directly to your Apple Files or iCloud Drive using standard iOS native file dialogs.
  • Google Drive Sync: If you connect Google Drive, the app uses direct OAuth 2.0 PKCE to write a single backup file directly to your personal Google Drive storage. Godspeed Grader developers never have access to your credentials or files.

4 Data Retention & Full Deletion Control

You retain full authority over your data. Under the Profile & Backup screen, you have access to:

  • Export: Download a full, readable JSON backup of your records at any time.
  • Clear All Workspace Data: Immediately wipe all local IndexedDB tables with a single tap.
  • Uninstalling: Deleting the app from your device immediately erases all locally stored database files.
