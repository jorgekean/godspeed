Visual Setup Walkthrough

Follow the 5 step-by-step videos below to set up classes, create exams, print bubble sheets, and grade papers with your camera.

Step 1 Quick Setup

Setup and Manage

Organize your school structure, grade levels, classes, and student rosters.

1
Go to Manage in the bottom tab to create your Grade Levels and Sections (e.g. Grade 10, Section Rizal).
2
Add your Subjects (e.g. Math, Science, English).
3
Add students with unique numbers, or import your whole class roster at once via Excel or spreadsheet copy-paste!
💡
Computer/Web Tip: Go to godspeedgrader.com/students on your computer to drag & drop your Excel file (.xlsx, .csv) or copy-paste directly from Google Sheets. Everything syncs to your phone!
📱 Setup & Manage Demo (0:43)
Step 2 1-Minute Exam

Creation of Exam

Create your exam and tap in your answer key in seconds.

1
Tap + Create New Exam on the Home dashboard.
2
Enter your exam title (e.g. Midterm Math), select Grade Level and Subject.
3
Choose 10, 20, 50, or 100 questions.
4
Tap the bubbles to input your Answer Key (A, B, C, D) and tap Save Exam.
📱 Exam Creation Demo (0:50)
Step 3 Print Sheets

Downloading Answer Sheet

Generate and print optical bubble sheets on standard printer paper.

1
Tap the Sheets tab in the bottom bar.
2
Select Generate 20-Item, 50-Item, or 100-Item Sheet.
3
AirPrint directly from iPhone or visit godspeedgrader.com on your laptop to print to a desktop printer.
4
Print Rule: Print at 100% scale on regular Letter or A4 paper (do not shrink to fit).
📱 Answer Sheet Demo (0:56)
Step 4 1 Sec / Paper

Scanning / Grading

Grade tests automatically in 1 second per paper using your device's camera.

1
On Home screen, tap your exam card and select the target section.
2
Tap Scan to activate the high-speed camera scanner.
3
Align all 4 corner square markers inside the guide box.
4
The app scores automatically with zero button presses needed!
📱 Live Scanning Demo (1:13)
Step 5 Instant Analytics

Reports / Item Analysis

Instantly review score analytics, question difficulty, and export grades.

1
Exam status automatically updates to GRADED on the dashboard.
2
Tap the Analytics icon to view class average, score distribution, and question accuracy.
3
Instantly spot the hardest questions to guide your next review session.
4
Export grades directly to CSV / Excel for your school portal.
📱 Reports & Item Analysis Demo (0:32)
💡 Pro-Tips for Perfect Scanning
• Lighting: Ensure even overhead light without harsh diagonal shadows across the 4 corner squares.
• Flat Paper: Smooth out any deep folds or curled edges so all 4 markers sit in a flat plane.
• Dark Markings: Works with standard HB/2B pencils and dark black or blue ballpoint pens.
